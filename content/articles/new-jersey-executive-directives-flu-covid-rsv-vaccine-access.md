The New Jersey Department of Health on September 29 issued executive directives to ensure access to flu, COVID-19, RSV and routine childhood vaccines during the 2026–2027 respiratory season.

Directive 26-002 recommends a COVID-19 vaccine for anyone 6 months and older, and a standing order lets pharmacy professionals give FDA-approved COVID-19 vaccines to anyone 3 or older without a prescription. Directive 26-003 adopts the American Academy of Pediatrics' 2026 child schedule and the American Academy of Family Physicians' 2026 adult schedule.

Under state law (P.L.2026, c.283), the commissioner's vaccine recommendations guide coverage without cost-sharing by state-regulated insurers. "I encourage every New Jerseyan to make an appointment today to stay up to date on their vaccines," Commissioner Raynard Washington said.
