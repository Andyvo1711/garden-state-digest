Gov. Mikie Sherrill and Health Commissioner Dr. Raynard Washington received their 2026–2027 flu vaccines October 13 at a Hudson Regional Health Commission Vaccines for Children clinic at the Bayonne Public Library, the state said.

Flu activity in New Jersey is currently low but higher than at this time last year. The Department of Health recommends an annual flu vaccine for everyone 6 months and older, with a high-dose vaccine for adults 65 and older when possible. Flu, COVID-19 and RSV shots can be given at the same visit.

Pharmacists can give COVID-19 vaccines to people 3 and older without a prescription, and the Vaccines for Children program provides free vaccines to eligible children through age 18. "Getting a flu shot takes just a few minutes," Washington said.
