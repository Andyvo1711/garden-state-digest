New Jersey payrolls fell by 1,700 jobs in August, to a seasonally adjusted 4,359,600, while the unemployment rate dipped 0.1 point to 4.3%, according to preliminary data released September 17 by the state Department of Labor and Workforce Development.

The private sector gained 200 jobs in the month, led by leisure and hospitality (+3,400) and construction (+1,000), offset by losses in professional and business services (-3,500) and private education and health services (-1,400). The public sector lost 1,900.

Over the year, total nonfarm employment was down 9,900 and private-sector employment down 13,100, though private education and health services added 22,900 jobs. July was revised to a loss of 21,900 jobs.

A later revision, published October 15, turned August's change into a gain of 1,200 jobs.
