The New Jersey Economic Development Authority (NJEDA) announced October 16 that it has invested $16.5 million to support eight innovative New Jersey startups through the New Jersey Innovation Evergreen Fund.

According to Trade & Industry Development, the approvals since February span advanced materials and clean technology to life sciences, technology and other emerging sectors. In one example, Elligint Health was approved for a $1.5 million investment, matched by HLM Investment Partners.

The Evergreen Fund is a state program designed to invest in startups alongside private investors. The full list of the eight companies and the individual amounts is available in the NJEDA's release.
