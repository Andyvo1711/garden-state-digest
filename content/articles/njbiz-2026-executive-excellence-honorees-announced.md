NJBIZ on August 27 announced nearly 60 of New Jersey's most accomplished senior business leaders as honorees of its 2026 Executive Excellence Awards.

A panel of independent judges selected the honorees using a points system after an open nomination process. Names listed in the announcement include Tom Angelo, CEO of HBK CPAs and Consultants; Joseph Jedlowski, chairman and CEO of Distinctive Living LLC; and Dr. Vasantha Kondamudi, executive vice president and chief medical officer of Holy Name Medical Center.

The awards ceremony is set for October 13 at The Palace at Somerset Park. The inaugural event took place October 14, 2025, at the same venue.
