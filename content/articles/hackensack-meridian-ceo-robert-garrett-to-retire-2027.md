Hackensack Meridian Health announced September 21 that CEO Robert Garrett plans to retire June 30, 2027. The board is conducting a national search for his successor and expects to choose one by the end of the first quarter of 2027.

Garrett would move into an advisory role as CEO Emeritus starting July 1, 2027, for up to a year to assist the board and the new CEO. He began at Hackensack University Medical Center in 1981, served as co-CEO of the system until 2019, then became sole CEO. The system now includes 18 hospitals and 41,000 employees.

Garrett said he had considered retirement for a few years and that 2027 "felt like a good time." In October, NJBIZ featured Garrett reflecting on his tenure.
