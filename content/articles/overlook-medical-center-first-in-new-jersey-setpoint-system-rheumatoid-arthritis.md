Atlantic Health's Overlook Medical Center in Summit announced on September 28 that it is the first hospital in New Jersey to use the SetPoint System, a neuromodulation device for rheumatoid arthritis (RA).

The SetPoint System, from SetPoint Medical, is a small implanted device that stimulates the vagus nerve to activate the body's innate anti-inflammatory pathways, giving patients a non-medication option.

Overlook has been first in the state with other technologies this year, including GE HealthCare's StarGuide SPECT/CT imaging system, announced in June.
