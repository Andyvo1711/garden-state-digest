Ridge Hair Studio held a ribbon cutting September 21 at 108 S. Finley Ave. in Basking Ridge, Patch reported September 30. Owner Leah Romer has 18 years of experience as a hairstylist.

The salon offers color, cutting and styling, smoothing and texturizing treatments, bridal services and more.

Bernards Township Committee member Jennifer Asay said Romer "brings 18 years of experience as a hairstylist to this exciting new chapter," adding that welcoming new businesses is a priority for the township.
