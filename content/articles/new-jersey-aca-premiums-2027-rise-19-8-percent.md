Individual-market health insurance premiums in New Jersey will rise by a weighted average of 19.8% in 2027, according to the state Department of Banking and Insurance, following a 15.9% increase for 2026. NJ Spotlight News reported the figures October 19. The figures are before financial assistance.

Increases vary by insurer: UnitedHealthcare 35.7%, Oscar 29.3%, Ambetter 20.8%, AmeriHealth 17.0% and Horizon 15.3%. Enrollment stood at 434,700 in June, and about 75,000 people lost coverage after the last open enrollment period.

Rising medical and prescription drug costs and the end of enhanced federal subsidies at the end of 2025 are driving the increases. Open enrollment runs November 1, 2026, through January 31, 2027.

"New Jersey families are already grappling with a serious affordability crisis," Gov. Mikie Sherrill said.
