Three New Jersey institutions placed among the top 100 on Forbes' 2027 Best Colleges list, with Princeton University ranking No. 2 nationally, according to a September 13 roundup by NJ Family.

Rutgers University–New Brunswick and the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT) in Newark also made the top 100. Published ranks for the two public universities differ between summaries of the list, so readers should consult Forbes' full table for exact placements.

The Forbes list arrived within weeks of two other 2026 college rankings that highlighted New Jersey schools. TIME's new "America's Best Colleges" list, released August 31, ranked Princeton No. 4 and NJIT No. 95.

Princeton, in Mercer County, is New Jersey's oldest and highest-profile private research university.
