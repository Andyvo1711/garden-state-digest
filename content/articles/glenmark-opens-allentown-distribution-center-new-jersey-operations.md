Glenmark Pharmaceuticals opened a roughly 160,000-square-foot distribution center in Allentown, Pennsylvania, that consolidates its U.S. shipping into one site, Pharmaceutical Technology reported October 30.

FleetOwner describes it as the company's largest distribution center in North America, and Area Development says the project is expected to create 45 jobs. One report says the facility was relocated from New Jersey and carries FDA and DEA approvals.

The company has a New Jersey footprint: 2026 job postings refer to Glenmark distribution centers in Mahwah, and sources place its corporate headquarters in Elmwood Park or Mahwah. Reports reviewed did not confirm whether the Mahwah operations have closed or moved.
