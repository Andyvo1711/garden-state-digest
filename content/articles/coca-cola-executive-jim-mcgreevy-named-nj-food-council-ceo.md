The New Jersey Food Council's board selected Coca-Cola executive Jim McGreevy as its next president and CEO, the group announced September 24.

McGreevy leaves his post at Coca-Cola, where he led the company's federal advocacy efforts in Washington, D.C. He succeeds Linda Doherty, who announced in February that she would retire in January 2027 after 33 years with the council, 23 of them as president and CEO.

Doherty said she looks forward to working with him during the transition and called his selection the start of a new chapter for the council.
