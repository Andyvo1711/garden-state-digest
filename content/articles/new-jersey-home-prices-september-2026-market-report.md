A September housing market report from a Pennsylvania–New Jersey brokerage, published October 15, puts New Jersey's statewide single-family median price at $614,000, with 3.1 months of supply and closed sales down 5.8% from a year earlier.

Zillow's Home Value Index, which measures all home types, shows the typical New Jersey home at $575,123 in September, up 4.0% year over year. The two figures aren't directly comparable.

Monmouth County's median price climbed 5.2% to $810,000 though closed sales fell 2.6%, and Hunterdon County's median rose 23.5% to $839,400. Mercer County's median was $435,000, down 6.5%. Realtor reports put Jersey City's median sold price at $801,000, a new quarterly high.

Local realtor reports use different methods, so figures vary by source.
