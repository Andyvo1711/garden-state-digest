Rutgers School of Nursing–Camden and the Rutgers University Foundation announced on August 12, 2026, that Johnson & Johnson is supporting a new workforce development initiative designed to expand access to nursing education in New Jersey.

The announcement, published in TAPinto's "Building Healthier Communities in New Jersey" column, describes the effort as expanding access to innovative nursing education through a new workforce development initiative.

Rutgers School of Nursing–Camden is part of Rutgers University's Camden campus in South Jersey.

Program details, including the amount of the support, were not included in the published summary of the announcement.
