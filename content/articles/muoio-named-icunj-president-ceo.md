The Independent Colleges and Universities of New Jersey (ICUNJ) announced in late August that former State Treasurer Elizabeth Maher Muoio will be its next president and CEO, effective September 8.

She succeeds Steve Reynolds, who has led the association for five years. ICUNJ advocates for the state's 13 private, nonprofit institutions of higher education.

Muoio served as New Jersey's treasurer from 2018 to early 2026. She holds a bachelor's degree from Wesleyan University and a law degree from Georgetown University Law School.
