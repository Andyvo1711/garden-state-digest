The New Jersey Innovation Institute (NJII), an NJIT corporation, appointed chief operating officer Jennifer D'Angelo as interim president, succeeding Michael Johnson, NJBIZ reported on September 21.

The move comes as NJIT itself is under interim leadership: John Pelesko became interim president of the university on August 4 after President Teik C. Lim's retirement.
