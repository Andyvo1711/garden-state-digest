Champi, a head spa rooted in the Ayurvedic tradition of head massage, held its grand opening October 23 at 467 Main St. in Metuchen, with Mayor Jonathan Busch on hand for the ribbon cutting, Patch reported.

The owners, Supriya Malhotra and Vaida Domeikyte, met through their sons, who are school friends, and later started the business together. "Champi" is the Hindi word for head massage, and the spa focuses on head and scalp relaxation.

"If you've never visited a head spa, think of it as a chance to slow down and unwind, with the focus on your head and scalp," Busch wrote on social media. "Congratulations, Supriya and Vaida, and thank you for choosing Metuchen."
