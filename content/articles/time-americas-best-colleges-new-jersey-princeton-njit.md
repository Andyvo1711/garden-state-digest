Princeton University and the New Jersey Institute of Technology are New Jersey's top-ranked private and public universities in TIME's inaugural "America's Best Colleges 2026-2027" list, published August 31.

Princeton placed No. 4 among 500 ranked schools nationally. NJIT came in at No. 95 overall and No. 38 among public institutions. Seventeen New Jersey colleges made the list, including Stevens Institute of Technology (No. 30), The College of New Jersey (No. 99), Montclair State University (No. 149) and Rutgers (No. 157).

Produced with data firm Statista, the ranking is built around one question: does a degree pay off? Student outcomes such as graduate earnings, graduation rates and return on investment account for 75% of the score, with learning environment at 15% and attractiveness to prospective students at 10%.

That approach reshuffles some familiar orders. Stevens, tied with NJIT in U.S. News, ranks well ahead of it in TIME's list, and Rutgers–New Brunswick, ranked No. 42 by U.S. News, lands at No. 157.
