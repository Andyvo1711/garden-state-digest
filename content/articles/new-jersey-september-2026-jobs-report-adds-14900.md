New Jersey payrolls rose by 14,900 jobs in September, lifting seasonally adjusted employment to 4,377,400, while the unemployment rate fell 0.1 percentage point to 4.2%, the state Department of Labor and Workforce Development reported October 15.

Private education and health services (+7,200), professional and business services (+7,100) and leisure and hospitality (+5,500) led gains. Trade, transportation and utilities (-5,100), information (-1,100) and the public sector (-1,100) declined.

Compared with a year earlier, total nonfarm jobs were up 3,600 and private-sector jobs up 6,900, with private education and health services adding 30,000. Trade, transportation and utilities lost 11,200 jobs over the year.

August was revised to a gain of 1,200 jobs from a preliminary loss of 1,700. October estimates are scheduled for release November 19.
