Hackensack Meridian Health reached a definitive agreement to acquire Hunterdon Health, which operates the 184-bed Hunterdon Medical Center in Flemington, according to an October 21 report. The purchase price was not disclosed, and federal and state regulators must still approve the transaction.

Hackensack Meridian plans more than $230 million in improvements: $200 million for facilities and access expansion and $30 million to add Epic's electronic health record system.

Hunterdon keeps its medical staff leadership and local board oversight for six years after closing and will have a representative on Hackensack Meridian's board. The Hunterdon Healthcare Foundation stays separate, and locally raised funds remain in the area.

"Hunterdon Health has a long history of excellent, community-centered care," said Hackensack Meridian CEO Robert C. Garrett. Hunterdon CEO Kristy Alfano said the agreement keeps local leadership and community input at the heart of how the system serves neighbors.
