The New Jersey Department of Health on August 7 announced awardees of its Hot Shots for Tots Immunization Campaign, a voluntary, point-based program recognizing child care and preschool facilities for immunization best practices.

Twenty facilities earned gold, eight silver and four bronze awards, and NORWESCAP Newton Head Start received an honorable mention. Points come from sharing immunization information with parents, attending an annual immunization workshop and participating in the New Jersey Immunization Information System.

"Staying up to date on vaccinations is crucial," said Dr. Tina Tan, the state epidemiologist. The department recommends the American Academy of Pediatrics schedule and encourages COVID-19, flu and RSV vaccines before the school year and cooler months.
