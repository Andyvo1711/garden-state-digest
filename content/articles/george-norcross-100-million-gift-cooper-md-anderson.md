Businessman George E. Norcross III announced July 28 that he will donate $100 million to Cooper University Health Care and MD Anderson Cancer Center at Cooper, in honor of his parents, George E. Norcross Jr. and Anne Carol Conner Norcross.

It is the largest philanthropic gift in Cooper's history and recognizes the family's 50-year relationship with the Camden system. In September, Cooper University Health Care changed its name to Cooper Norcross Health.

The gift arrives as Cooper pursues Project Imagine, a $3 billion expansion of its Camden campus that began in January 2025.
