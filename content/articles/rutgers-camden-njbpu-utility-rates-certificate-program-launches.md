Rutgers Business School–Camden and the New Jersey Board of Public Utilities (NJBPU) have launched the state's first Utility Rates Certificate Program, a workforce effort to prepare the next generation of utility rate experts. A release dated September 4 says the inaugural cohort includes 30 participants from public and private utilities, regulatory agencies and consulting firms.

The program is established through a three-year memorandum of understanding and pairs academic coursework with paid fellowships. Courses include macroeconomics, microeconomics, financing structures in the utility industry, and public utility industry and regulation, covering topics such as rate design, revenue requirements, cost-of-service analysis, rate base and rate of return.

No prior utility experience is required, and graduates may apply for up to six credit waivers toward Rutgers Business School–Camden's MBA or MSBA programs. Peter McAliney directs Rutgers–Camden's Professional and Executive Education unit, and finance professor Richard Michelfelder is involved in the program.
