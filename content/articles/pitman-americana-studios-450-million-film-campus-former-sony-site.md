A developer team of Americana Studios Holdings and SORA Northeast, working with the borough of Pitman, is advancing plans for a film and television production campus with a mixed-use district on the former Sony plant, according to a borough press release dated October 26.

The proposal, described at a borough council meeting as a $450 million project, covers 76 acres at 400 N. Woodbury-Glassboro Road in Gloucester County. It includes a production studio plus housing, a hotel, restaurants, a museum and public gathering spaces. Mayor Michael Razze told CBS most of the former Sony building would be demolished.

The release projects more than 400 permanent jobs, in addition to 400 temporary positions. If approved, the site would be built out over about three years. It has been largely idle since the plant closed in 2011, and final details are still being worked out.
