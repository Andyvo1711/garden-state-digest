New Jersey collected more than $10 billion in taxes from July through September, up nearly 19% from a year earlier, according to Treasury figures reported by NJ Spotlight News on October 16.

Income tax rose 11% for the quarter, sales tax about 12%, and the corporation business tax more than 80%. Treasury officials said the corporate gain is not fully indicative of future performance and described much of it as a one-month correction. September's 15% income tax jump falls to about 4% after adjusting for an extra withholding payment. Realty transfer fees were up more than 6% year to date, while lottery revenue fell nearly 8%.

The state is operating under a record spending plan of nearly $61 billion through June 30, 2027, and total revenues need to rise nearly 4% year over year to keep it on track. Officials also pointed to inflation, high gas prices and economic pressure tied to the war in Iran as risks.
