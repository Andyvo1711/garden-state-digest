Parsippany-based B&G Foods named Robert "Rob" D. Mills president and chief executive officer on August 10, replacing Kenneth "Casey" Keller, whose retirement took effect August 7 after five years in the role.

Mills has sat on the company's board since March 2018. He previously served about 12 years at Tractor Supply, most recently as executive vice president and chief technology officer, and held senior roles at Ulta Beauty and Sears.

Just Food reports the company's stated priorities are reshaping its portfolio, improving margins and cash flow, and reducing debt and leverage. Keller will assist with the transition.
