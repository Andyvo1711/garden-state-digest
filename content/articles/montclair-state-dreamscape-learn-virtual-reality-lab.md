Montclair State University announced September 1 the opening of a new virtual reality facility built in partnership with Dreamscape Learn, giving students immersive, hands-on ways to study.

The 2,450-square-foot space is in the College of Communication and Media building and includes a 26-seat classroom and a free-roam pod in which students physically move through virtual environments. It previously served as a video and broadcast production studio.

The lab is intended for the university's more than 24,000 students, with plans to expand course offerings over time, and aims to train students for careers in VR content creation. Arizona State University opened the first Dreamscape Learn lab in 2021; Montclair State cites its own finding that biology students in such courses were nearly twice as likely to earn an A on lab assignments.

News coverage of the opening appeared September 1–4 from outlets including NJBIZ, News12 and Campus Technology.
