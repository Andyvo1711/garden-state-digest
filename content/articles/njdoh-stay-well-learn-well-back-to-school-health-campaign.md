The New Jersey Department of Health on August 21 launched "Stay Well, Learn Well," a statewide campaign to connect families with vaccinations, physicals, screenings and wellness services before the school year.

An online Community Calendar lists vaccine clinics, screenings and family wellness events. Partners include Atlantic Health, Cooper University Health Care, Hackensack Meridian Health and RWJBarnabas Health, plus local health departments and federally qualified health centers.

Kindergarten vaccination coverage for MMR, DTaP and polio fell to about 92% nationally in 2025-26, the release said, and New Jersey's exemption rate rose to 5.7%, with MMR coverage below the 95% level needed to prevent measles spread. "Vaccinated kids are healthy kids," Gov. Mikie Sherrill said. Free community events ran through October.
