Among the distinctive courses New Jersey colleges are offering this fall, NJIT is teaching "Visions of the Promised Land: Bruce Springsteen's America," part of the Humanities and Social Sciences department's New Jersey Studies Initiative, according to an NJBIZ roundup of unusual fall 2026 offerings.

The course examines Springsteen's life, music and cultural influence as a lens for understanding American identity, civic responsibility and social justice. It is taught by Dan Loughran, an assistant superintendent in Franklin Township Public Schools and a published Springsteen scholar.

NJIT's announcement debuting the New Jersey Studies Initiative also points to coursework on Boardwalk Empire-era politics alongside Springsteen. Other campuses are adding their own fall courses, including Princeton classes on food culture and camera-free filmmaking.
