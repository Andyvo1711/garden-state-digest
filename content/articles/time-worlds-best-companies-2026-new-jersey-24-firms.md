Two dozen New Jersey companies made TIME's 2026 World's Best Companies list, released September 9, according to NJBIZ. The magazine's fourth annual edition covers 1,000 organizations.

TIME's separate America's Best Companies 2026 list, released in July, included 32 New Jersey companies. Merck ranked No. 9 overall, the highest-placing New Jersey company, and Commvault took the No. 1 spot among New Jersey-based companies in the technology and media category.

NJBIZ's article carries the full list of the 24 New Jersey firms.
