New Jersey placed 31st in CNBC's 2026 America's Top States for Business rankings, unveiled July 15, one spot lower than last year, and ranked last in the nation for business friendliness, according to NJBIZ and the New Jersey Business & Industry Association.

Education ranked 2nd and quality of life 3rd, while access to capital, workforce, and technology and innovation landed between 13th and 20th. Neighboring Pennsylvania ranked 13th, New York 18th and Connecticut 23rd. Ohio led the list, followed by North Carolina, Virginia, Texas and Minnesota.

Business groups seized on the result. At an October Assembly hearing, NJ Chamber of Commerce president Tom Bracken cited the 50th-place friendliness ranking as evidence that the state needs bolder policy changes.
