RWJBarnabas Health opened its sixth Food Farmacy on September 8 at Monmouth Medical Center in Long Branch, the first in Monmouth County. It is located in the hospital's Alexander Pavilion and joins five others in Middlesex, Mercer, Hudson and Essex counties.

The program, part of the system's Our Healthy Communities initiative, is run in partnership with Fulfill, the food security organization serving Monmouth and Ocean counties. It stocks fresh New Jersey-grown produce alongside refrigerated and shelf-stable items.

Patients also receive nutrition counseling from registered dietitians to support longer-term health.
