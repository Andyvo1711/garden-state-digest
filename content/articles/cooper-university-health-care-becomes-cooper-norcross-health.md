Cooper University Health Care announced September 23 that it has changed its name to Cooper Norcross Health following a $100 million gift from George E. Norcross III, the largest philanthropic donation in the system's history.

The gift, announced July 28 for Cooper and MD Anderson Cancer Center at Cooper, honors Norcross's parents, George E. Norcross Jr. and Anne Carol Conner Norcross, and marks the family's 50-year relationship with Cooper.

The system is midway through Project Imagine, a $3 billion expansion of its Camden campus launched in January 2025. Its first phase includes a 10-story hospital tower at MLK Boulevard and Haddon Avenue across from MD Anderson Cancer Center at Cooper; the full plan calls for three new patient towers with 745 all-private beds.
