John A. Pelesko, an NJIT alumnus and the university's provost and executive vice president for academic affairs, began serving as interim president of New Jersey Institute of Technology on August 4, 2026.

The Board of Trustees voted unanimously to appoint him on June 11, two days after NJIT announced that President Teik C. Lim would retire, effective August 3. The board's resolution describes the interim role as a bridge while a search for a permanent president is carried out.

Moshe Kam was named interim provost, also effective August 4, filling the post Pelesko held before moving into the president's office.

NJIT, based in Newark, is one of New Jersey's largest public research universities and was ranked No. 95 nationally in TIME's inaugural "America's Best Colleges" list released later in August.
