Health Commissioner Dr. Raynard Washington donated whole blood August 25 at New Jersey Blood Services' Freehold Donor Center to draw attention to a critically low statewide blood supply, the state Department of Health reported.

Donations typically decline in summer, and New Jersey Blood Services has declared two states of emergency this year after heat waves, severe storms and power outages cut donations. The American Red Cross has declared a national Emergency Blood Shortage. Types O-negative and O-positive are especially needed.

"Right now, we encourage every eligible New Jerseyan to commit just one hour of their time to give blood and save lives," Washington said.
