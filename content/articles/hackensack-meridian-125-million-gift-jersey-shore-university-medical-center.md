Hackensack Meridian Health announced September 14 that it received a $125 million gift to support expansion of Jersey Shore University Medical Center in Neptune, the largest philanthropic commitment in the system's history and the largest health system gift in New Jersey history.

The donor is anonymous. The gift honors John Lloyd, the system's former co-CEO, and a pavilion at the hospital will be named for him. NJBIZ reports Hackensack Meridian has committed more than $1 billion over 10 years to expand the hospital; a dedication ceremony has not been scheduled.

Jersey Shore University Medical Center is Monmouth and Ocean counties' regional referral hospital and part of an 18-hospital system.
