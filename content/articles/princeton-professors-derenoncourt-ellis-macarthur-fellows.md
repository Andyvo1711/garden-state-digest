Princeton University professors Ellora Derenoncourt and Elizabeth Ellis were named 2026 MacArthur Fellows on September 29. Each receives an $800,000 grant, paid over five years, with no restrictions on how it is used.

Derenoncourt, an associate professor of economics who joined Princeton in 2021, was recognized for "uncovering the historical roots of racial disparities in wealth and economic mobility in the United States."

Ellis, an associate professor of history, studies early North American and Native American history. She helps lead the Department of History's Delaware History Project, carried out in partnership with the Delaware Tribe of Indians and the Delaware Nation.

They are among 20 fellows in the 2026 class. Princeton graduate alumnus Kaiyu Guan '13, now at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, was also named a fellow.
