Parker Health Group announced September 25, after a national search, that Mark Lenhard will be its next president and CEO. He is expected to join the Piscataway-based aging services organization in December 2026.

Lenhard currently serves as president and CEO of United Methodist Communities and the UMC Foundation, which has appointed Bob Peterson as interim leader. He succeeds Roberto Muñiz, who plans to retire by the end of 2026 after nearly 28 years leading Parker.
