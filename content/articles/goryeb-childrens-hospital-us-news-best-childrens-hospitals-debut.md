Atlantic Health's Goryeb Children's Hospital in Morristown ranked third in New Jersey on U.S. News & World Report's 2026-27 Best Children's Hospitals list, released September 15, marking the hospital's first appearance on the list.

NJBIZ reports that Hackensack Meridian Health's Joseph M. Sanzari and K. Hovnanian Children's Hospitals and RWJBarnabas Health's Bristol-Myers Squibb Children's Hospital tied for No. 1 in the state. Goryeb had two ranked specialties, while each of the leading two systems had three.

U.S. News is marking 20 years of ranking children's hospitals this year. In all, three New Jersey hospitals made the list.
