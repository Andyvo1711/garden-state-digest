New Jersey hospitals committed about $7.8 billion to community health in 2024, according to the New Jersey Hospital Association's report "Health Starts Here: 2026 New Jersey Hospitals Community Impact Report," covered by the New Jersey Monitor on October 29.

The report covers 59 nonprofit acute care facilities and says 21% of every dollar they spend goes to community benefit programs, which include free care, no-cost screenings, and research. Community benefit spending included more than $5 billion in unpaid care and nearly $1.5 billion in subsidized health services, meaning care provided at a loss.

The total is well above earlier NJHA figures, including $7 billion reported for 2023; the 2025 report notes its totals are based on IRS filings, and differences in reporting years and methods likely explain part of the gap.
