Prudential Financial plans to sell two of its three Newark office buildings, Prudential Plaza and the 21-story building at 213 Washington Street, and consolidate its staff at Prudential Tower, 655 Broad Street, according to reports from September and October.

CEO Andrew Sullivan said several of the company's buildings are underutilized and that holding space it no longer needs has a cost. The transition is expected to take several years. Prudential says Newark will remain its global headquarters and that it has committed more than $1.2 billion to the city over the past decade.

JLL has been hired to market the properties as "The Newark Collection," covering about 1.147 million square feet combined. Jersey Digs reported that a state WARN notice shows 125 layoffs in Newark between November 28 and December 31.

Prudential Tower opened in 2015, developed by SJP Properties at a cost of $444 million with a $210 million NJEDA tax break.
