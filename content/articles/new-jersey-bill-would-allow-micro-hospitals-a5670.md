A bill introduced in the Legislature would allow licensed hospitals to create micro-hospitals, smaller, lower-cost facilities that offer primary and emergency care without a required operating room or surgical team, according to NJ Spotlight News (October 22–23).

Bill A5670 would cap micro-hospitals at 50 patient beds. New ones would have to be more than five miles from a competing acute care facility, while existing hospitals could convert or restructure a site with no location restrictions. The Department of Health would write the rules.

"We don't want to lose hospitals in rural and underserved communities," said Sen. Joe Vitale (D-Middlesex), chairman of the Senate health committee. The New Jersey Hospital Association has not taken a position, and the bill has not been scheduled for a hearing.
